**Iran (Reuters):** Oil rose 1% after Iran said no enriched uranium would leave the country; 37 ships were attacked in October, two tankers hit mines.

**Ukraine (Reuters):** Russia and Ukraine agreed in principle to a major prisoner exchange after two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi.

**DRC Ebola (Al Jazeera):** Cases passed 11,000 — 11,042 confirmed, 5,387 dead — from the Bundibugyo strain in eight provinces, the DRC's deadliest outbreak.

**Kenya (Reuters):** Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, starting a 42-day countdown to being declared free of the disease.

**COP31 (Al Jazeera):** UN chief Guterres told leaders in Antalya the climate crisis is "outrunning us" after record 2025 emissions.

**Israel (Deutsche Welle):** The new Knesset met after the deadlocked October 27 vote; Netanyahu's bloc holds 53 seats, Eisenkot's 48.

**Gaza (Al Jazeera):** Israeli strikes hit a tent camp in Gaza, killing five, as the UN appealed for tents for people facing floods.

**Spain (Reuters):** Four million workers joined Spain's first general strike in 14 years over housing, before the November 29 election.

**Spy arrest (CNN):** A 41-year-old US soldier was arrested near Gloucester on suspicion of spying for Russia, while serving with NATO.

**Yemen (Al Jazeera):** Government forces said 190 Houthi fighters were killed or wounded in six provinces; the WHO counts 1,771 killed since August.

**Hegseth (Reuters):** The House voted 211-204 to send the Hegseth impeachment bid to committee, effectively killing it.

**Mississippi (AP):** Democrat Scott Colom won the Mississippi Senate seat as Cindy Hyde-Smith conceded, the first Democratic win since 1982.

**Iran operatives (NPR):** US prosecutors indicted three men accused of being Iran-backed operatives behind nearly 20 attacks in Europe and the US.

**Wall Street (AP):** Stocks rebounded as CoreWeave jumped 24.7%; the S&P 500 rose 1.3% and Europe's STOXX 600 hit a record.
