**Iran War and Hormuz Shipping:** Ten shipping attacks occurred in November following an October record of 37 vessels struck. Two tankers burned after hitting naval mines Tuesday, and another was struck off Oman. U.S. Senator Marco Rubio said economic pressure removes the need for major combat, while Tehran insists the Strait of Hormuz stays closed until its seven conditions are met.

**Ukraine-Russia Peace Talks:** U.S., Russian, and Ukrainian negotiators concluded a second day of Abu Dhabi talks, agreeing on a major prisoner-of-war exchange. Concurrently, Russian strikes hit six electricity substations across Ukraine, and NATO scrambled jets after drones entered Baltic airspace.

**Ebola Outbreak in DR Congo:** DRC's Ebola outbreak reached 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths across eight provinces. Meanwhile, Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, marking a major milestone toward virus-free status.

**COP31 Climate Summit:** UN chief António Guterres warned at COP31 in Antalya that the climate crisis is "outrunning us" after record 2025 emissions, urging an end to fossil fuels. British PM Andy Burnham is the sole G7 leader attending.

**Spain General Strike:** Tens of thousands joined Spain's first general strike in 14 years over housing and living costs, disrupting Barcelona ahead of November 29 elections.

**Israel Deadlock:** Israel's new parliament was sworn in following a deadlocked election without a majority for Netanyahu. Meanwhile, Israeli strikes in Gaza killed at least seven people.

**Spy Arrest in Britain:** A 41-year-old U.S. Army soldier was arrested in Gloucester on suspicion of spying for Russia.

**Yemen Conflict:** Government strikes hit Houthi positions across six provinces, with WHO reporting 1,771 killed since August.

**US Politics:** The House killed an impeachment bid against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, while Democrat Scott Colom won a Mississippi Senate seat, a historic first in 140 years.
